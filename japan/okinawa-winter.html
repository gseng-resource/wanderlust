Okinawa in Winter

Three nights on Okinawa's main island in December: a snorkel in the Blue Cave, the Churaumi aquarium, Shuri Castle's newly reopened hall, and Okinawan food from Agu pork to market fish.

Researched November 2026 for a mid-December trip. Opening hours and prices change, so check before you go.

What December is like

Air around 18–21°C, dry but windy; the sea is about 23–24°C. You can snorkel and dive with a wetsuit, and winter visibility is the best of the year (20–35 m), but it is not beach-lounging weather. Prices are off-peak, except for the 29 Dec–3 Jan spike. Humpback whales arrive from late December and are best in late January to February.

Check flight days first. Some direct routes into Naha run only two or three days a week, which fixes the shape of the trip. This plan assumes landing early afternoon on day 1 and leaving early afternoon on day 4.

Itinerary: 4 days, 3 nights

Day 1
Land at Naha around 14:00, collect the car, drive about an hour north to Onna. Cape Manzamo at sunset.
Night: Onna
Day 2
08:00 Blue Cave snorkel at Cape Maeda, about 2 hours, wetsuit included. Afternoon free: a coast walk or a second swim if the weather holds.
Night: Onna
Day 3
Churaumi Aquarium from opening (8:30) to about 11:30. Drive south (about 2 hours) to Shuri Castle on a timed slot. Evening in Naha.
Night: Naha
Day 4
Tsuboya pottery street and the market in the morning. Airport by about 11:45.

If the north wind cancels the boat on day 2, swap: aquarium that day, snorkel on the morning of day 3. Prefer one base? Stay in Chatan all three nights; it adds about 40 minutes each way to the water.

Where to eat

Day 1

  • Late lunch: many local places close 15:00–17:00, so stop at A&W Makiminato on Route 58 (root beer and burgers, 06:00–02:00) or Gordie's in Chatan (11:00–20:00).
  • Dinner: Shimabuta-ya Maeganeku, steamed Agu pork, ¥4,000–6,000. Book; closed Wednesdays and Sundays.

Day 2

  • Breakfast: hotel buffets open around 07:00, which is tight for an 08:00 snorkel. Buy pork-egg onigiri at a konbini the night before.
  • Lunch: Sea Side Drive-in, five minutes from Maeda, soup, taco rice and fish burgers, ¥1,000–2,000 (closed Wed). Or the stalls at Onna no Eki.
  • Dinner: Nakaya, Agu pork and rafute with live sanshin music, ¥4,000–5,000. Phone ahead.

Day 3

  • Lunch: Kishimoto Shokudo in Motobu, wood-ash Okinawa soba since 1905, about ¥1,000 (closed Wed; arrive by 11:30 to beat the queue). Or Soba-ya Yoshiko (closed Tue and Fri).
  • Dinner: Makishi Public Market: buy fish on the ground floor and have it cooked upstairs, ¥3,000–5,000 (open daily in December). Or Urizun for Okinawan classics; book.

Day 4

  • Breakfast: Itsudemo Asagohan on Kokusai Street, Okinawa soba and juushii, from 07:00.
  • Lunch: Pork Tamago Onigiri at Naha Airport. There is a branch in the domestic arrivals hall and one on 4F of the international terminal.

Sights at a glance

  • Blue Cave, Cape Maeda: tours run in winter, wetsuit included. Parking is ¥100 an hour. North wind can cancel boat trips.
  • Churaumi Aquarium: 8:30–18:30, last entry 17:30, ¥2,180.
  • Shuri Castle: the main hall reopened on 23 November 2026 after the 2019 fire. Going inside needs a timed reservation.
  • Nakagusuku Castle ruins: UNESCO-listed, 8:30–17:00, ¥500.
  • Sakima Art Museum: the Marukis' huge Battle of Okinawa mural, ¥900, closed Tuesdays.
  • Sefa-utaki: reported largely closed for conservation from 2 November 2026 to 5 January 2027.

Driving

  • Foreign licences need a 1949 Geneva-type International Driving Permit, carried with the original licence.
  • Rough drive times: Naha Airport → Onna 60–70 min · Onna → Churaumi 50–60 min · Churaumi → Shuri about 2 h · Naha → airport 15–20 min.
