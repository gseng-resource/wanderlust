Why Okinawa in winter

The sea and the Ryukyu kingdom in one short trip: a snorkel in the Blue Cave, the Churaumi whale sharks, and Shuri Castle’s hall, newly rebuilt after the 2019 fire.

In December: air around 18–21°C, sea about 23–24°C, dry but windy. Snorkelling works with a wetsuit and visibility is the best of the year, but it is not beach-lounging weather. Off-peak prices except 29 Dec–3 Jan; humpback whales from late December.

Best time to go: April to early May and October to November are best: warm sea, settled weather and fewer typhoons. December to March is the cool season, good for whale watching (January to March) and clear water but not for beach days. Avoid the mid-May to late June rainy season, the July–September typhoons, and Golden Week.

The route: Naha Airport → Onna (2 nights) → Motobu and the north → Naha (1 night). 4 days and about 260 km by car; 3 days and about 220 km from a single base in Onna.

Highlights

  • Blue Cave snorkel at Cape Maeda
  • Churaumi Aquarium, the Bise fukugi tree lanes and the Kouri Island bridge
  • Nakijin Castle ruins (UNESCO)
  • Shuri Castle’s reopened hall, with the Shurijo exhibition at the Prefectural Museum
  • Okinawan food: Agu pork, wood-ash soba, fish cooked at the Makishi market

Good to know

  • Check flight days first: some direct routes into Naha run only two or three days a week.
  • North wind can cancel the Blue Cave boat, so keep a swap day.
  • The Prefectural Museum closes on Mondays and Sakima on Tuesdays.

Two ways to do it

💰 Best value: 3 days, 2 nights, one base

  • Stay: both nights at Best Western Okinawa Onna Beach (about CNY 690 a night), right by the Blue Cave. No hotel move.
  • Do: the Blue Cave snorkel, Churaumi, the Bise tree lanes and the Kouri bridge, then Shuri Castle on the way to the airport.
  • Eat: Okinawa soba at Kishimoto, Sea Side Drive-in, market fish at Makishi.
  • About CNY 2,100–3,050 per person excluding flights.

✨ Best experience: 4 days, 3 nights

  • Stay: two nights at Homm Stay Yumiha (9.4, private villa with a pool, about CNY 980 a night, near Cape Maeda) or Kariyushi Resort EXES Onna (9.1, beachfront, about CNY 1,020). Last night at Hewitt Resort Naha (rooftop pool, about CNY 575).
  • Do: a private Blue Cave tour, Nakijin Castle, Kouri, the Shurijo exhibition at the Prefectural Museum with Shuri Castle's reopened hall, and Yachimun no Sato for pottery.
  • Eat: Okinawan izakaya dinner at Onna Tsubaki, sanshin night at Nakaya, Okinawan classics at Urizun.
  • About CNY 3,650–4,600 per person excluding flights.

Without a car

Okinawa has no Shinkansen or long-distance rail. The only train is the Yui Rail monorail in Naha; everything else is by bus or tour.

  • Yui Rail: Naha Airport, Kencho-mae and Makishi (Kokusai Street), Omoromachi (prefectural museum, DFS, Main Place), Shuri (castle). 1-day pass ¥1,000 (CNY 40), 2-day ¥1,800 (CNY 75); a day cap applies when you pay by contactless card. Every 6–12 min.
  • Airport → Onna hotels: the Airport Limousine Bus, about 1h30–1h45, from about ¥1,530 (CNY 65).
  • To Churaumi: the Yanbaru Express bus, about 2h30, around ¥2,000 (CNY 85); or a day tour from Naha (Churaumi, Kouri, Bise) for about ¥5,000–8,000 (CNY 210–330).
  • Verdict: workable for Naha, Shuri, one Onna resort and a Churaumi day tour. The capes, Kouri, Nakijin and the south take many slow connections, so a car is much better in December.

Top sights

  • Blue Cave, Cape Maeda: tours usually run in winter, wetsuit included; parking ¥100 (CNY 5) an hour. North wind or swell can cancel boats, or let them out but keep them out of the cave; check whether your tour includes transport to the meeting point.
  • Churaumi Aquarium: 8:30–18:30, last entry 17:30, ¥2,180 (CNY 90).
  • Shuri Castle: the main hall reopened on 23 November 2026 after the 2019 fire; going inside needs a timed reservation.
  • Nakijin Castle ruins: UNESCO-listed walls on a ridge near Churaumi, 8:00–18:00, about ¥1,000 (CNY 40).
  • Kouri Island: a 2 km bridge over very clear water. Free.
  • Bise fukugi tree lanes: an old village lined with fukugi trees, beside Churaumi. Free.
  • Tamaudun: UNESCO royal tomb, five minutes' walk from Shuri Castle, about ¥300 (CNY 15).
  • Shikinaen: UNESCO royal garden in Naha, 9:00–17:30, closed Wednesdays, about ¥400 (CNY 15).
  • Peace Memorial Park and Museum: the main memorial to the Battle of Okinawa, in the south, 9:00–17:00, ¥300 (CNY 15).
  • Okinawa World and Gyokusendo cave: a 5 km limestone cave and craft village, 9:00–17:30, ¥2,000 (CNY 85).
  • Cape Manzamo and Zanpa Cape: coastal cliffs for sunset.
  • Winter lights: the Southeast Botanical Gardens illumination runs through the winter (garden to 22:00).
  • Sefa-utaki is reported largely closed for conservation from 2 November 2026 to 5 January 2027.

On in December 2026

  • Okinawa Prefectural Museum: Shurijo: the Royal Castle of the Kingdom, 1 Dec 2026–31 Jan 2027, ¥1,200 (CNY 50). A natural pair with the reopened castle.
  • Okinawa Prefectural Art Museum: collection exhibition with Yamashiro Kenshin and Okinawan art currents, to 28 Mar 2027, ¥400 (CNY 15).
  • Sakima Art Museum: a Rouault print show (Miserere, Les Fleurs du mal) announced in November; end date not published. The Marukis' Battle of Okinawa mural is always on.
  • Ocean Expo Park: a winter special is announced but undated. The Naha City Museum of History is closed for rebuilding.

Book; open daily from 17:00, walkable from Maeganeku. Or Sanshin no Hana Tabelog 3.47 · 400+ reviews with live sanshin music; phone bookings only (098-989-5515), free hotel pick-up.

Day 2

  • Breakfast: hotel buffets open about 07:00, tight before an 08:00 snorkel; buy pork-egg onigiri at a konbini the night before.
  • Lunch: Sea Side Drive-in Tabelog 3.49 · 700 reviews near Maeda, soup and taco rice, ¥1,000–2,000 (CNY 40–85) (closed Wed). Or Onna no Eki market stalls.
  • Dinner: Nakaya Tabelog 3.40 · 144 reviews, Agu pork with live sanshin music, ¥4,000–5,000 (CNY 170–210). Phone ahead.

Day 3

  • Lunch: Kishimoto Shokudo Tabelog 3.68 · 1,407 reviews in Motobu, wood-ash soba since 1905, about ¥1,000 (CNY 40) (closed Wed; arrive by 11:30). Or Soba-ya Yoshiko Tabelog 3.54 · 185 reviews (closed Tue and Fri).
  • Dinner: Makishi Public Market: buy fish downstairs, have it cooked upstairs at Kiraku Tabelog 3.44 · 108 reviews, ¥3,000–5,000 (CNY 130–210). Or Urizun Tabelog 3.49 · 1,166 reviews for Okinawan classics; book.

Last day

  • Breakfast: Itsudemo Asagohan Tabelog 3.47 · 255 reviews on Kokusai Street, from 07:00 (or the hotel, on the 3-day plan).
  • Lunch: Pork Tamago Onigiri at Naha Airport Tabelog 3.48 · 1,100+ reviews (domestic branch), in the domestic arrivals hall and on 4F of the international terminal.

Shopping

  • Kokusai Street: souvenirs, Ryukyu glass and bingata textiles, roughly 10:00–22:00.
  • Makishi market and the Heiwa-dori arcades: local food, dried goods, snacks.
  • Tsuboya Yachimun-dori: yachimun pottery shops, about 10:00–18:00.
  • Yachimun no Sato, Yomitan: pottery bought direct from the kilns.
  • San-A Naha Main Place, Omoromachi: food gifts and awamori, 9:00–22:00 daily.
  • T Galleria Okinawa (DFS), Omoromachi: duty-free, 10:00–20:00.
  • AEON Mall Okinawa Rycom: the island's biggest mall, 10:00–22:00.
  • American Village, Chatan: fashion and sea-view cafés.
  • Ashibinaa Outlet near the airport: outlet brands, 10:00–20:00.
  • Onna no Eki: local produce, tempura and shaved ice in Onna.

Driving

  • Foreign licences need a 1949 Geneva-type International Driving Permit, carried with the original licence.
  • The Okinawa Expressway saves time going north; tolls are roughly ¥700–1,000 (CNY 30–40) per run.
  • Total driving: about 260 km over four days, or about 220 km over three. Allow extra time around Naha at rush hour.
