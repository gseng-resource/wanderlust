About this research

How the Wanderlust guides are put together, where the information comes from, and how far you can rely on each part.

How a guide is made

  • Each guide starts as private trip research. A guide is published only once its itinerary, stays, food, sights, transport and budget are complete.
  • Guides are trip-specific, never person-specific: no travel dates, home city, party details, bookings or private notes.
  • Every guide is published in English and Simplified Chinese with the same content, and both are taken down together if a guide is withdrawn.
  • Nothing is sponsored. There are no affiliate links, and links are saved as plain addresses with no tracking.

Ratings

  • Hotels: Booking.com guest scores out of 10, as seen on the check date.
  • Restaurants: Tabelog scores, shown as a small badge with the number of reviews. On Tabelog 3.0 is average, 3.5+ very good and 3.6+ excellent; a score on few reviews means little data, not poor food. Restaurants were picked first for being on the route, open on the days that matter and mid-priced; the score is shown for transparency. Scores were read from Tabelog search listings on 26 November 2026, because Tabelog blocks automated page loads.

How budgets are worked out

  • Per person, for two people sharing a room and a car.
  • Flights are not included, because they depend on where you start.
  • Included: hotels at the snapshot prices, car hire (from rental listings), expressway passes or tolls, fuel, meals at the suggested places, and entry fees.
  • Not included: shopping, travel insurance, mobile data, parking and checked-bag fees.
  • Yen are converted at ¥1 ≈ CNY 0.042 (rate of 26 November 2026), and yen prices show the CNY equivalent in brackets. Ranges show the cheaper and dearer choices in each guide.
  • "Best value" and "best experience" use the same route; only the hotels, meals and tours change.
